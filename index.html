How do I reserve my stay?

Choose “Reserve Your Stay” and add your dates and guest details. The form sends your enquiry directly to our reservations team. Our team will reply with availability and rates, and confirm your booking directly.

Where is Lotus Villa located?

Lotus Villa is in Daksum, Kashmir. Contact our team before travelling for the exact location, directions, and help planning your arrival.

Can you help us plan a day out?

Tell us which places you would like to explore, and our team can help with itinerary suggestions. Mountain roads and activities depend on the season and local weather, so check conditions before setting out.
